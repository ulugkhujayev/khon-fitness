# 90/90 stretch (ninety)

## 1. What this stretch targets

Assume left leg in front, right leg behind, as in the app cues.

Front left hip is flexed 90 degrees, abducted, and externally rotated. Primary lengthening is left gluteus maximus, left piriformis and other deep external rotators (obturator internus, obturator externus, gemellus superior, gemellus inferior, quadratus femoris), and posterior hip capsule. Secondary lengthening is left gluteus medius and minimus, mainly posterior fibres.

Back right hip is internally rotated, knee bent 90 degrees. Primary lengthening is right iliopsoas, rectus femoris, tensor fasciae latae, and anterior capsule. Secondary is right adductors.

Trunk and pelvis are stabilised by rectus abdominis, obliques, transversus abdominis, erector spinae, bilateral gluteus medius and minimus, and adductors.

## 2. Correct form, step by step

1. Sit tall after a short warm-up. Both sit bones down, hips square, shoulders level.
2. Front left leg: thigh forward, knee 90 degrees. Rest outer knee, outer calf, and ankle edge on the floor. Calf roughly parallel to torso, ankle neutral.
3. Back right leg: thigh out to the side 90 degrees from torso. Knee 90 degrees, lower leg pointing straight back. Rest inner knee, inner calf, and ankle on the floor.
4. Level the pelvis and avoid leaning sideways.
5. Hinge from the hips over the front shin. Keep the spine long. Do not round the low back.
6. Breathe in to lengthen, breathe out to settle. Hold 20 to 60 seconds per side, then switch. Use 1 to 2 minutes for cool-down.

## 3. Common form errors and how they change what gets stretched

Rounding the low back loads spinal ligaments and reduces glute stretch. Leaning or lifting one sit bone unloads one hip. Forcing a lifted front knee down stresses the knee, not deep rotators. Letting the back leg slide forward loses internal rotation and hip flexor length. Twisting instead of hinging shifts work to obliques. Holding the breath or pushing through pinch hides poor position.

## 4. Audit of the app's current cues against sources

1. Set up: "Sit on the floor with your knees bent and your feet planted." Verdict: reword. It is generic, not a tall square base. Replacement: "Sit tall with both sit bones down and legs extended forward."
2. Front leg: "Lower your left knee. Bring that shin across your body, with the knee bent to 90 deg." Verdict: reword. Add outer-leg contact. Replacement: "Lower left knee out front, knee bent 90 degrees, calf on floor."
3. Back leg: "Fold your right leg behind you, also at 90 deg. Rest both legs on the floor." Verdict: reword. "Behind you" is vague. Replacement: "Fold right leg to the side, knee bent 90 degrees, inner leg down."
4. Lean: "Keep your chest long. Lean from your hips over your front shin." Verdict: keep. Add breath. Replacement: "Lengthen spine, hinge at hips over front shin, breathe deeply."

Missing: hold 20 to 60 seconds, keep hips square, stop if pinch or knee pain, repeat other side.

## 5. Audit of the current highlight

What is wrong: coral is a small sliver on the shorts at one hip. It marks clothing, not muscle. It hides during motion and misses the two-sided hip action. It stays fixed while emphasis shifts.

The highlight should change between steps.

- Frame 0 set up: no strong glow. If used, show faint trunk stabilisers only.
- Frame 21 front leg: glow left posterior hip and buttock: left gluteus maximus, left piriformis and deep rotators, left gluteus medius and minimus. Do not glow shorts seam, knee, low back, or right leg.
- Frame 42 back leg added: keep left glow, add right anterior hip and groin: right iliopsoas, right rectus femoris and tensor fasciae latae. Do not glow outer thigh, knee cap, foot, or spine.
- Frame 63 lean: increase left posterior glow, keep right anterior glow lower. Do not glow lumbar spine, shoulders, or knees.

## 6. Pose audit

Fold the back right leg so the thigh is 90 degrees from the torso, knee bent 90 degrees, inner knee and calf down, lower leg pointing back, foot relaxed. Align right knee near hip height. Set front left leg with outer knee and calf down, knee 90 degrees, calf parallel to torso, ankle neutral. Keep both sit bones down, pelvis level, hips square, torso tall. Hinge from hips with a straight spine. Do not round, lean sideways, or lift a sit bone.
