# Elephant walks (key: elephant)

## 1. What this stretch targets

Dynamic active stretch of the posterior chain, one leg at a time. Assume frame 32 bends left, frame 63 bends right.

Primary, straight leg only: hamstrings (biceps femoris long head, semitendinosus, semimembranosus) and gastrocnemius (medial and lateral heads); straight knee plus heel down drives both.

Secondary: erector spinae, gluteus maximus, and soleus to a lesser degree.

Contract or stabilise: straight-leg quadriceps (rectus femoris, vastii) to lock the knee; tibialis anterior for dorsiflexion; transverse abdominis, obliques, erector spinae for trunk brace; gluteus medius to keep hips square.

## 2. Correct form, step by step

Stand feet hip-width apart, toes forward. Hinge at hips with a flat back and place hands on floor, blocks, or bench if needed.

Bend one knee about 10 to 30 degrees and lift that heel, while the other knee locks at 0 degrees with heel down. Drive the hip back over the straight leg and tense that quadriceps.

Hold 1 to 3 seconds, then switch slowly. Keep hips square and motion slow. Do 20 to 25 reps per side.

Breathe normally, never hold the breath. Go to tension, not pain. Use it as a warm-up; ACE places dynamic work before training and 15 to 30 second static holds after.

## 3. Common form errors and how they change what gets stretched

Rounding the low back shifts load from hamstrings to spine. Flat back rotates the pelvis forward and pulls hamstrings long; round back leaves them slack and stresses discs.

Bending both knees or leaving the straight knee soft unloads hamstrings and gastrocnemius. The calf work then falls to soleus.

Keeping the bent-side heel down weakens the one-leg isolation. Lifting it isolates the straight leg.

Bouncing, rushing, rotating the pelvis, sliding the hands, overarching the low back, or craning the neck all change the target and raise strain risk.

## 4. Audit of the app's current cues against sources

1. "Fold forward and place your hands on the floor." Reword. Omits hinge, flat back, feet, block option.
Replacement (12 words): "Stand feet hip-width. Hinge at hips. Place hands on floor or blocks."

2. "Keep your hands down. Bend one knee while the other leg lengthens." Fix. "Lengthens" is vague, omits heel, lock, quad squeeze.
Replacement (13 words): "Lift left heel, bend left knee. Straighten right leg and squeeze right thigh."

3. "Slowly straighten that leg as you bend the other knee." Reword. Omits side, squeeze, hold.
Replacement (12 words): "Straighten left leg and squeeze left thigh as you bend right knee."

Add missing shared cue: hold 2 seconds, breathe normally, keep hips square.

## 5. Audit of the current highlight

Wrong: glow stays on one hamstring while knees alternate, so half the reps show the wrong leg. It also shows hamstring only and misses the calf.

Highlight must change each step and follow the straight leg.

Keyframe map, assuming frame 32 bends left and frame 63 bends right:

Frame 0 (both legs long, hands down): glow both posterior thighs and both upper calves, low level. No quads, shins, back, arms.

Frame 32 (left bent, right straight): glow right posterior thigh and right upper calf. No left hamstring, left calf, quads, or back as primary.

Frame 63 (right bent, left straight): glow left posterior thigh and left upper calf. No right hamstring, right calf, quads, or back as primary.

Optional faint glow at all frames: erector spinae and gluteus maximus. Never mark bent-leg hamstring or straight-leg quadriceps as stretched.

## 6. Pose audit

Show a clear bend difference. Bent knee flexed with heel up, straight knee at 0 degrees with quad on. Small bend on both knees is wrong.

Keep hips square, no rotation or side shift. Push straight-side hip back.

Hold a long spine with anterior pelvic tilt, no lumbar round. Gaze down, neck neutral.

Fix hands and feet. Hands stay planted while legs switch. Feet hip-width, straight foot flat, bent heel raised.

Hold each side 1 to 3 seconds. Do not sway continuously.
