# Thread the needle (needle)

Left-side version: left arm threads under right arm.

## What this stretch targets

Primary muscles lengthened, left side: posterior deltoid, infraspinatus, teres minor, teres major, rhomboids, middle trapezius, latissimus dorsi, and thoracic rotators. The cross-body reach adducts and internally rotates the left shoulder. The rib-cage turn loads thoracic rotation.

Secondary: left levator scapulae and upper trapezius, left posterior capsule, and right pectoralis major and anterior deltoid if the right arm reaches forward.

Stabilisers: right deltoid, triceps, serratus anterior, and rotator cuff; rectus abdominis, obliques, gluteals, and quadriceps hold hips over knees; neck extensors hold the head without loading it.

## Correct form, step by step

1. Start on hands and knees. Hands under shoulders. Knees under hips, hip-width. Spine neutral, gaze down.
2. Exhale and slide the left arm under the right arm, palm up. Reach long.
3. Rest the left shoulder and left ear, cheek, or temple on the mat. Do not load the head. Keep hips over knees and square.
4. Keep the right hand planted, or walk it forward for more stretch. Let the upper back widen.
5. Breathe into the back of the ribs. Hold 20 to 30 seconds, or 5 to 10 breaths. Do 2 to 4 reps for about 60 seconds total. Repeat on the right.

General guidance: 10 to 30 seconds per hold, 30 to 60 if older, to mild tightness, with normal breathing.

## Common form errors and how they change what gets stretched

Hips drift back or twist open. This unloads the thoracic twist and shifts load to the lumbar spine or hips.

Weight on the head or forehead. This loads the neck and unloads the posterior shoulder.

Threaded arm palm down, bent short, or held off the mat. This cuts horizontal adduction, so infraspinatus, teres minor, and posterior deltoid stretch less.

Support shoulder collapses inward. This narrows the upper back and limits rhomboids and middle trapezius stretch.

Motion from low back or neck, with hunch or sag. This replaces thoracic rotation with lumbar or cervical strain.

Holding the breath or forcing range. This raises tension and blocks release.

1. "Start on hands and knees. Put your hands under your shoulders." Keep, but add knees and spine. Suggested text: "Start on hands and knees. Hands under shoulders, knees under hips." (13 words)
2. "Slide your left arm under your right arm, palm facing up." Keep, but add breath and reach. Suggested text: "Exhale and slide your left arm under your right, palm up." (12 words)
3. "Lower your left shoulder and the side of your head to the mat. Keep your hips over your knees." Keep, but name the contact point and add breathing and hold. Suggested text: "Rest left shoulder and ear on mat. Keep hips over knees. Breathe." (13 words)

Add a fourth cue for hold time and side change, since sources give a range and all sources repeat both sides.

## Audit of the current highlight

What is wrong: the constant "centre upper back plus threaded-arm deltoid" glow misses the left-side bias, marks the whole deltoid instead of the posterior shoulder, and omits the rotator-cuff, periscapular, latissimus dorsi, and thoracic-rotation targets. Constant glow is wrong because stretch rises from frame 0 to 63.

Highlight should change: off or faint at setup, partial during the thread, full at hold.

Keyframe map, left-side version:

Frame 0, set up: no stretch glow. Neutral table. If stabilisers must show, use faint neutral on the trunk, not coral.
Frame 32, thread: light coral on left posterior shoulder, left shoulder-blade border, and mid-thoracic spine. Left side only.
Frame 63, hold: full coral on left posterior shoulder, left between-shoulder-blade area, left side-back, and mid-thoracic spine. Add left neck edge only if a neck cue is added.

Should not glow: left anterior deltoid, biceps, or chest; lumbar centre; hips, thighs, knees, calves; right deltoid, except as neutral support, never in coral.

## Pose audit

Side of head, not top: left ear, cheek, or temple down, gaze right, no forehead press, no weight on the neck.

Left shoulder fully down: back of shoulder on the mat, arm long, palm up, not hovering or bent short.

Hips over knees: hips lifted above knees and square, shins hip-width. Do not sit back or shift sideways.

Right support arm vertical: right hand under shoulder, shoulder open. Do not collapse inward.

Spine: flat mid-back with thoracic twist, soft low back, no hunch, no sag.
