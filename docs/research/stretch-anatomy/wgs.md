# World's greatest stretch (wgs), left side

## 1. What this stretch targets

Front leg left, back leg right.

Primary lengthened: right iliopsoas and right rectus femoris from back-leg extension; right calf (gastrocnemius, soleus); left gluteus maximus and proximal hamstrings in the lunge; left adductors when the elbow drops inside; in rotation, left pectoralis major, left anterior deltoid, left external oblique, intercostals, and thoracic rotators.

Secondary: left ankle, thoracic extensors, rhomboids, trapezius, serratus posterior superior, left erector spinae.

Contract or stabilise: right gluteus maximus and right quadriceps hold the back leg straight; transverse abdominis and obliques keep hips square; right serratus anterior and infraspinatus stabilise the support shoulder.

## 2. Correct form, step by step

Start in high plank, hands under shoulders, core braced.

1. Step left foot outside left hand. Front knee about 90 deg, shin vertical, knee over ankle. Right leg straight, knee off floor, on ball of foot. Hips square, spine long.
2. Drop left elbow inside left foot. Keep right hand flat. Sink hips without sagging.
3. Plant right hand inside left foot, inhale, rotate chest left, reach left arm up. Eyes follow hand. Turn from upper back.
4. Exhale, return left hand to floor.

Dose: dynamic 3 to 5 seconds per spot or 30 seconds per side, 10 to 12 reps, 2 to 3 sets. Static hold 20 to 30 seconds per side. ACE cites 30 seconds to 2 minutes for static holds.

## 3. Common form errors and how they change what gets stretched

- Knee passes toes, shin tilts: loads front knee, unloads hip and ankle.
- Back leg bends or hips sag: shortens right iliopsoas and rectus femoris, removes hip flexor stretch.
- Hips open with chest: pelvis follows, thoracic and oblique stretch drops.
- Arch from low back: stresses lumbar spine, misses chest and upper back.
- Neck cranes, eyes down, bouncing, breath held: less opening, less control.

## 4. Audit of the app's current cues against sources

1. "Step your left foot beside your left hand. Keep the back leg long." Reword, misses outside foot, knee, square hips. Replacement: "Step left foot outside left hand, knee over ankle. Keep right leg straight." (15 words)
2. "Bring your left elbow toward the inside of your front foot." Keep, add support hand. Replacement: "Plant right hand, drop left elbow inside left foot. Keep hips square." (13 words)
3. "Turn your chest left and reach your left hand up. Follow it with your eyes." Keep, name support. Replacement: "Plant right hand, open chest left, reach left arm up. Eyes follow." (13 words)

Same-side elbow-down then reach-up is valid and matches sources. Main gap is the missing right support hand and joint landmarks.

## 5. Audit of the current highlight

Wrong: both glutes, both quads, and whole upper back glow from frame 0 with no change. Bent left quadriceps is shortened, not stretched. Right gluteus maximus contracts, it is not stretched. Upper back has no rotation load yet.

Yes, highlight should change between steps.

- Frame 0, set up: glow right iliopsoas, right rectus femoris, right calf, left gluteus maximus, left hamstrings. No glow: left quadriceps, chest, shoulders, upper back.
- Frame 32, lower: keep frame 0, add left adductors and left mid-back paraspinals. No glow: whole upper back both sides, chest, reaching arm.
- Frame 63, rotate: keep right hip flexors, add left pectoralis major, left deltoid, left obliques and intercostals, left thoracic muscles. No glow: right chest and arm, both quads, both glutes, full back sheet.

## 6. Pose audit

- Put left foot outside left hand, shin vertical, knee over ankle, not past toes.
- Keep right leg straight, knee lifted, heel back, right glute on.
- Keep hips square, spine long. No rounded upper back, no arched low back.
- Plant right hand flat inside left foot in rotate. Left hand cannot support and reach at once.
- Reach left arm near vertical, open chest left, eyes to left hand, turn from upper back.
- Pull left toes toward shin for ankle dorsiflexion.
