# Hamstring stretch - fold

Bilateral standing forward fold. Both legs stretch at once.

## What this stretch targets

Primary, both legs: biceps femoris long head, semitendinosus, semimembranosus. All three cross hip and knee, so hip flexion with a near-straight knee lengthens them. Biceps femoris short head crosses only the knee, so it is not a main target.

Secondary, both legs: gastrocnemius, gluteus maximus, lumbar erector spinae with fascia. Calf tension rises with straight knees and neutral ankles. Back gets gentle traction, not a prime stretch.

Contract or stabilise, both sides: quadriceps femoris to hold knees long without locking, iliopsoas and rectus abdominis to flex hips and draw trunk to thighs, tibialis anterior and foot muscles for balance, gluteus medius with tensor fasciae latae to keep kneecaps forward. Spinal extensors brake the descent, then relax at the bottom.

## Correct form

1. Stand feet hip-width apart, outer edges parallel, toes forward. Weight even through heels and balls of feet. Spine tall.
2. Inhale and lift chest. Exhale and tip pelvis forward at the hip crease. Lead with chest, keep spine long.
3. Hands to shins, blocks, or floor. Knees soft, more bend if tight. Thighs gently firm, kneecaps lift. Head hangs, neck long.
4. Breathe slowly. Lengthen on inhales, soften on exhales. No bounce, no forcing.
5. Hold 20 to 30 seconds, or 5 to 10 breaths, 2 to 4 rounds. Sources give 10 to 30 seconds for most adults, 30 to 60 for older adults, about 60 seconds total per muscle. Stop at mild tension, not pain.
6. Exit with bent knees, hands on hips, flat back to stand.

## Common form errors

- Rounding from waist, not hips. Loads lumbar discs and shifts work off hamstrings.
- Locking knees. Moves pull to back of knee and sit-bone attachment, raises strain risk.
- Chasing the floor. Pulling with arms or craning neck turns a leg stretch into back stress.
- Bouncing. Fires stretch reflex and risks biceps femoris strain.
- Holding breath or hunching shoulders. Blocks trunk release over legs.
- Weight in heels, pelvis drifting back. Stops forward pelvic tilt and weakens hinge.

## Audit of current cues

1. "Stand with your feet hip-width apart." Keep, add foot place. Replacement (13 words): "Stand tall, feet hip-width apart, toes forward, weight even through both feet."
2. "Bend at your hips and reach your hands toward the floor." Fix, it cues rounding. Replacement (19 words): "Hinge forward at the hip crease with a long spine, knees soft, hands to shins or floor."
3. "Relax your neck. Keep a small bend in your knees if needed." Reword, add breath and time. Replacement (19 words): "Let head hang, neck long. Keep knees soft. Breathe slowly and hold 20 to 30 seconds."

## Audit of current highlight

Wrong: hamstrings plus lower back glow coral at all times, even standing. Standing upright does not lengthen hamstrings. Lower back is not a prime target, and lighting it rewards rounding. A constant glow hides the rest to stretch change.

Should highlight change: yes. Off at rest, rising in fold, full at hold.

Map, both sides:

- Frame 0, stand: glow: none. No glow: hamstrings, calves, gluteals, lumbar spine, arms, neck.
- Frame 32, fold: glow: posterior thigh, mid strength; light calf. No glow: lumbar spine, neck, shoulders, arms, front of thighs.
- Frame 63, hold: glow: posterior thigh, full strength; light calf and gluteal fold. No glow: lumbar spine as stretched muscle, neck, arms, quadriceps.

Keep back in a neutral tone if needed, never coral.

## Pose audit

- Frame 0: neutral pelvis over ankles, spine tall, knees soft, arms at sides. Remove lean, knee lock, back glow.
- Fold entry: pelvis rolls forward, sit bones lift up and back, spine stays long first. No lumbar-led rounding.
- Knees and feet: visible micro-bend at frames 32 and 63, kneecaps forward, hips square, feet parallel. No hyperextension.
- Head and arms: head hangs, gaze to shins, shoulders drop. Hands to shins or blocks if floor breaks spinal line.
- Weight: over midfoot to balls of feet, belly toward thighs. No heel dump.
