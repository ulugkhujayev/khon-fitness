# Khon Fitness 0.4.7

The nine stretch guides now show a continuous male figure with a flatter chest,
modeled hands and feet, and dark shorts from the navel to just below the knees.
Each guide has two camera views. The animations keep planted feet in contact
with the floor and show the intended pose through the numbered steps.

Muscle highlights follow the active step and remain visible over the shorts.
Several poses and cues were corrected, including the hip flexor tuck, 90/90
knee bends, Elephant walks heel lift, and Plow support cue. The player keeps
its explicit Start control, countdown, pause behavior, and offline playback.

The signed APK is `dist/khon-fitness-0.4.7-knee-shorts.apk`.
