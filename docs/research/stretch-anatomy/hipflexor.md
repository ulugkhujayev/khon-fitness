# Hip flexor stretch (hipflexor)

Note on side: app cues kneel on right, so stretch side is right. Review as right-side stretch.

## What this stretch targets

Primary: right iliopsoas (psoas major and iliacus), deep in front of hip from lumbar spine and iliac fossa to lesser trochanter. It is the main hip flexor lengthened in hip extension.
Secondary: right rectus femoris (hip-crossing quad), tensor fasciae latae, and sartorius. Rectus femoris stretch is mild here because rear knee stays flexed near 90 deg without added knee flexion.
Must contract or stabilise: right gluteus maximus drives posterior pelvic tilt, abdominals hold ribs down and pelvis level, left quad and glute hold half-kneel support. Glute and core are active, not stretched.

## Correct form, step by step

1. Set up half-kneel: right knee under right hip on pad, left foot flat ahead with left knee about 90 deg over ankle. Hips square forward, torso tall, hands lightly on front thigh or hips.
2. Tuck pelvis: squeeze right glute and roll tailbone under into slight posterior tilt. Keep ribs down, no back arch. Many feel stretch here before moving.
3. Shift slightly: glide hips 1 to 3 inches forward while keeping tuck and tall chest. Stop at clear tension in front of right hip.
4. Breathe and hold: breathe normally, deepen slightly on each exhale without losing tuck. Hold about 30 sec per side, 2 to 4 rounds. ACE gives 30 to 45 sec for 2 to 5 reps; NASM gives about 30 sec for lengthening.

## Common form errors and how they change what gets stretched

Arching low back into anterior tilt keeps hip flexors slack and loads lumbar spine and anterior hip capsule instead of iliopsoas.
Lunging far forward loses tuck, pushes front knee past toes, and shifts load to front knee and back.
Leaning chest over front thigh closes hip angle and removes stretch.
Letting front knee fall inward stresses knee and changes hip line.
Relaxing right glute loses posterior tilt and turns stretch into back extension.
Holding breath or forcing into pain causes guarding and blocks lengthening.

## Audit of the current highlight

Wrong: constant front quad plus glute glow. Mid-quad belly is not primary target, and gluteus maximus contracts to tilt pelvis so it should not show coral "stretched" color. Constant glow also hides progression from setup to stretch.
Highlight should change between steps: none or minimal at setup, then increasing hip-front glow as tuck and shift add length.
Keyframe map:
Frame 0 setup: no coral glow. Body is in position but not yet stretched.
Frame 32 tuck: light coral on right front hip crease (iliopsoas) and thin upper front thigh (proximal rectus femoris and TFL). No glow on right glute, left leg, low back, or lower quad.
Frame 63 hold: stronger coral on same right hip-front regions as shift adds tension. Same no-glow regions as frame 32.
If app shows contraction, use a separate color for right glute, never coral.

## Pose audit

Tuck and Hold look same as Setup, so user sees no cue to copy. Add visible posterior tilt at frame 32: tailbone under, lower back flattens, right hip moves slightly forward.
Add small forward hip glide at frame 63: 1 to 2 inches, front knee stays near 90 deg over ankle, rear knee stays under hip.
Keep torso vertical with ribs down and hips square at all frames; do not arch or lean chest forward.
Fix rear hand: rest lightly on front thigh or hip for balance, not pressed hard into thigh, which reads as pushing and tips torso forward.
Make frame differences clear: neutral pelvis at 0, tucked at 32, tucked plus forward at 63.
