# Plow pose (plow)

## What this stretch targets

Plow is bilateral. All muscles below mean both sides.

Primary muscles lengthened: erector spinae (spinalis, longissimus, iliocostalis), deep posterior spinal muscles (multifidi, semispinalis, splenius capitis and cervicis), gluteus maximus, hamstrings (semitendinosus, semimembranosus, biceps femoris), gastrocnemius and soleus. Gravity adds load to mid to low back paraspinals in a way seated folds do not.

Secondary muscles lengthened: pectoralis major and minor, anterior deltoid, coracobrachialis and serratus anterior when shoulders tuck under.

Muscles that must contract or stabilise: rectus abdominis, transversus abdominis and obliques; iliopsoas and quadriceps (rectus femoris, vasti) to hold legs straight; tibialis anterior and toe extensors; rhomboids, middle and lower trapezius and levator scapulae; infraspinatus and teres minor; posterior deltoid, teres major and triceps.

## Correct form, step by step

1. Lie supine. Shoulders on 2 to 3 folded blankets, head on floor off blankets. Arms by sides, palms down. Legs together.
2. Exhale, brace abdomen, lift legs to 90 degrees at hips with knees straight.
3. Without momentum, lift hips and carry legs overhead. Support low back with hands if needed.
4. Set feet only as far as control allows: floor, wall or chair. Knees straight, feet active, hips over shoulders, spine long.
5. Tuck shoulders under, press arms into floor or interlace fingers. Weight on upper back, not neck. Gaze up. Do not turn head.
6. Breathe easy. Hold 15 to 30 seconds or 5 breaths at first; build to 3 to 5 minutes. Roll down one vertebra at a time.

## Common form errors and how they change what gets stretched

Throwing legs over loads the neck and removes abdominal control.
Forcing toes to floor collapses the spine and shifts load to the neck and lumbar discs.
Letting hips sit behind shoulders unloads the back and increases neck pressure.
Turning the head under load risks disc and nerve injury.
Flat neck without blankets increases cervical compression.
Dangling feet removes stability and reduces posterior chain stretch.
Bent knees reduce hamstring and calf stretch.
Wide elbows and untucked shoulders load the neck.
Holding the breath or exiting fast stresses the spine.

## Audit of the app's current cues against sources

1. Set up: keep. Clear and matches sources. Replacement: "Lie on your back, arms by sides, palms down, shoulders on blankets."
2. Lift: keep but add control and gaze. Replacement: "Exhale, brace core, lift straight legs to vertical. Look up."
3. Roll: reword to forbid momentum and allow hand support. Replacement: "Slowly roll hips up and carry legs overhead. No swinging."
4. Hold: fix. Do not cue forced reach, and name neck safety. Replacement: "Rest toes on floor or support. Weight on upper back, not neck."

All replacements are under 20 words.

## Audit of the current highlight

What is wrong: constant lower back plus hamstrings is partly right but incomplete. It misses gluteals, calves, thoracic extensors and anterior shoulder stretch, and it implies the same stretch in all frames.

Highlight should change between steps. All glows below are bilateral.

Frame 0 set up: no stretched-muscle glow. Optional faint neutral base only. Should not glow: back, legs, shoulders, neck.
Frame 21 lift: light glow on hamstrings (semitendinosus, semimembranosus, biceps femoris), gastrocnemius and soleus. Should not glow: neck, upper back, shoulders, arms.
Frame 42 roll: medium glow on hamstrings, gluteus maximus, lumbar and thoracic erector spinae. Light glow on calves. Should not glow: neck front, arms, chest.
Frame 63 hold: strong glow on hamstrings, gluteus maximus, gastrocnemius and soleus, thoracic and lumbar paraspinals. Light glow on pectoralis major and minor, anterior deltoid and posterior neck extensors. Should not glow: contracting core, quadriceps, iliopsoas, rhomboids, infraspinatus, triceps, face, front of neck, hands.

## Pose audit

Hips must stack over shoulders with a long spine. Do not show hips behind shoulders.
Show weight on upper back with neck space and normal curve, not flat loaded neck.
Keep head centered, gaze up, chin slightly in but not jammed.
Show legs together, knees straight, feet active. Do not show forced toe touch with buckled back.
Show shoulders tucked, arms pressed down, elbows narrow, palms down or interlaced.
Add blanket height under shoulders and a foot support option; hovering feet are valid.
