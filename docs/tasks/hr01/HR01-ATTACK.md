## 3. Verdict

**open-ended.** Time overlap can hide an unrelated workout and can authorize irreversible deletion. A valid session ID is also insufficient to identify the row or range an asynchronous read began with. Close both paths before building.

- **Coverage and deletion.** COMMON 118–135 uses the same half-of-shorter rule for a display filter and a delete. A two-minute workout entirely inside a long gym session passes at 100% of the shorter range. Even a unique same-type overlap can be a different workout. For automatic hiding, require verified app and band bounds, matching known activity types, a unique match, starts and ends within five minutes each, and at least half of **both** ranges. Treat five minutes as a proposed tolerance for the owner to approve. Keep cross-type and other uncertain cases visible. Retain raw record IDs through grouping. Replace D4's unconditional cross-type hide. For deletion, use overlap only to find proposed pairs. Require independent identity evidence or explicit owner confirmation, one-to-one pairing, and a checked plan that preserves user facts. The baseline stores imported IDs only on the imported copy and does not prove a link to an app session. The current automatic D9/D10 deletes cannot be justified with those fields. Keeping both rows is the safe default. Pair copies also have session watts, stroke, notes and potentially different round semantics. A note starting with “From ” can be user text. Preserve such text unless the app can identify its origin. **READ** data/Entities.kt:77, :123 and data/Repository.kt:345–352.
- **Windows.** COMMON 55–74 infers live work from a date check, form timestamps and nullable duration. A manual entry can pass the same date and 60% rules. A late set check can also turn a gym session into a day-long read. Persist the captured local day and use immutable, trusted start and end anchors. Use [from, to), reject inverted and future bounds, and require at least 60 seconds. For gym, use valid done stamps inside the session, cap at Finish or last check plus five minutes, and enforce the four-hour bound with or without checks. For legacy gym, require independent evidence of the original local day and trusted clock bounds. Keep old sessions without that evidence eligible for a user-confirmed range only. Editable dates are calendar labels. An intentional move away from the recorded day disables the automatic inference. For timed cardio, use a measured finish or Stop anchor even when the form is open. With round bounds, cap the read at last work end plus 60 seconds and at the measured stop. Keep the six-hour upper bound on inferred cardio. Remove the unfinished timeSec plus 15 minutes estimate. A short band record can still be an import choice without becoming an automatic HR window. Start-day filtering in Add to may use a stored local day. Coverage uses epoch ranges across midnight. Derive timer boundary times from elapsed time and a wall anchor with a same-boot check. A clock discontinuity or missing legacy anchor leaves the affected bounds uncertain. **READ** data/Repository.kt:267–280, health/HealthImport.kt:38 and timer/TimerSnapshot.kt:12–16.
- **Sample quality and diagnostics.** COMMON 36–46 and 83–98 asks ten raw samples to establish a complete result. Duplicates or a short burst pass, and bucketing loses actual first/last times and sample gaps. Apply the same paged and clipped reader for fill, Add to and diagnostics. Choose a deterministic trusted origin stream, deduplicate exact observations, and keep raw read and accepted counts separate. Require at least ten accepted timestamps, with at least one in each outer quarter, for a whole-session summary. Apply a separate documented coverage rule per round, such as two distinct timestamps spanning at least half the round. Retain sparse-read facts with the coverage outcome. Calculate averages, maxima and raw diagnostic gaps before bucketing. Use ordered, unique accepted timestamps for diagnostic gaps and report zero or one timestamp with no gap value. C16's age transition follows a successful sparse read. A failed read never consumes the retry. Keep distance optional and require matching source and workout evidence. **READ** health/HealthImport.kt:106–115 and :128–135.
- **Commit and refresh.** COMMON 93–94 rechecks existence. Replace can recreate the same ID, and an edit can change its input range without deleting it. Use one repository commit rule with the current session and database generations, effective range, interval revision, source claims and relevant exercise fields. Check those in the write transaction after the provider read and before any result write. Keep user-owned fields, including deliberate blanks, separate from reader-owned values. A refresh can replace reader-owned values and invalidate obsolete ones. Replace E6's drop-before-read. One process-level reader should queue changed work even when another read is in progress. Commit timer duration and bounds together and clear a finished snapshot only after acknowledgement. **READ** data/Dao.kt:232–241, :554–568, data/Repository.kt:46–51 and timer/TimerService.kt:175–179, :281–289.
- **Fixed range and recovery.** P2 27–32 puts the authoritative user choice in a cache row that P3 can drop. Store explicit bounds, link type and all claimed Health Connect IDs as durable session data with uniqueness and cleanup. Plan its schema and backup representation in P1. Keep lookup state independent. The app session remains app-origin after attachment or duplicate removal. A startup flag outside Room cannot prove which pairs the safety file covers. Keep a Room pass journal, immutable safety snapshot identity and pair revisions. Publish the private file before deletion, recheck those revisions at commit, and exclude restored data from the old pass. Provide a local restore route. **READ** data/Dao.kt:209, :271–275, ui/screens/HistoryRules.kt:222 and ui/screens/PlanScreen.kt:404–424, :478–481.
- **On-phone data and choices.** COMMON 139–142 prohibits suggestions and off-phone data while preserving an import that infers an exercise from the plan and has a bulk save. It also adds HR to a database with platform backup enabled and to an export sent to a user-selected document provider. Resolve both conflicts in the contract. Keep automatic copies and HR-bearing files on-device. Keep the explicit exercise picker and remove inferred selection if the no-suggestion rule has no exception. The existing updater is a separate path and must receive no workout data. **READ** src/android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml:23–29, ui/screens/PlanScreen.kt:416–421, health/HealthImport.kt:80–84 and ui/screens/ImportScreen.kt:94–106.
- **Protocol conflict.** P3 calls itself Normal and specifies deleting stored HR during Read again. The review protocol puts stored-data rewrites in Risky. Put the data mutation and its proof in the risky engine contract. Have P3 call it. **READ** inputs/5-HR01-P3-ui.md:3–5, :39–48 and review-protocol.md:17–25.
